# Tile 6 Spec: Post-Core Vertical

## General Tile Identity
- **Tile Name**: Tile 6
- **Tile Type**: **Tertiary** (Urban center transitioning to a logistics zone)
- **Primary Visuals**: Continuing from **Tile 5’s dense urban core** with very tall skyscrapers on the left, gradually transitioning into **mid-rise buildings** and a **major shopping mall** featuring **gothic/cathedral-like architecture**. The right side begins transitioning into **Logistics and Airport Zones**.

## Key Features

### 1. Urban Continuity
- **Left Edge**: The skyline continues from **Tile 5** with **very tall buildings** that anchor the city’s **urban core**.
- **Mid-section**: As the tile progresses to the right, building height **gradually reduces** while overall urban intensity remains high: skyscrapers and very tall towers on the left transition into dense mid-rise blocks, commercial slabs, apartment towers, and transportation/civic structures.
- **Right Edge**: Begin the **transition** to **Logistics and Airport Zones**, reducing urban density.
- Skyline envelope must decline from LEFT to RIGHT without regrowing a centered or center-right crest.
- Tile 6 should feel like post-core release, not like a second crowned skyline.
- The decline should be continuous; avoid any secondary apex, re-crown, or center-right skyline hump before Tile 7.
- Keep substantial density in the foreground and lower-mid band, especially on the left and center-left.
- Do not let Tile 6 become a low-rise foreground with the main city mass only in the distance.

### 2. Shopping Mall (Gothic Cathedral-Like Architecture)
- **Design**: A grand **shopping mall** with **gothic arches**, **stonework**, **high vaulted ceilings**, and **glass domes**. It should evoke the feel of a **monumental civic/cathedral-like urban building**, avoiding a boxy commercial look.
- **Location**: Central in Tile 6, acting as a **visual anchor** and **cultural landmark**.

### 3. Train/Subway Terminal
- **Train Terminal**: A massive **train terminal** in the **center-right**, resembling **Grand Central Station** or **St. Pancras Station**, with **grand arches**, **passenger platforms**, and a **major transportation hub**.
- Alternatively, a **subway terminal** with **underground entrances** could be placed, adding to the **urban functionality**.

### 4. Urban Features
- **Luxury Apartments/Hotels**: High-end **residential towers** or **luxury hotels** near the transition zone.
- **Plaza or Civic Center**: A **public space** with **cultural landmarks**, like a **city hall**, **public art**, or **museum**.

### 5. Transition to Airport/Logistics
- **Right side** of Tile 6 begins the **transition** to **Logistics Zones** and **airport**. The buildings should be **less dense**, with more **functional, low-rise structures** like **warehouses**, **cargo centers**, and **transportation infrastructure**.

### 6. Dry Urban Core (hard)
- Tile 6 must remain **dry**.
- **No visible river, coastline, harbor, bay, canal, or distant open-water horizon read.**
- Do not allow far-background blue bands or reflective water planes that read as coast/river.
- Any reflective surfaces must read as **glass, roofs, paved surfaces, or rail/terminal infrastructure**, not water.

### 7. Optional/Occasional Placemark Ideas
These elements are **optional** and should be used sparingly, only when the scene needs extra variety without overcrowding the composition:
- **Civic Center or Plaza**: A **large plaza** with **public art installations** or **government buildings** like a city hall or **museum**. This could act as a **cultural and civic center** for Tile 6, creating a **visual break** between the urban sprawl and offering a **public space** in the otherwise dense, retail-focused area.
- **Luxury Apartments or Hotel Towers**: Near the transition area between tall buildings and smaller ones, you could introduce **luxury apartments** or **high-end hotels**. These would add to the **city’s vertical character** while being a bit **less dense** and more **exclusive**.

## Seam & Stitching Details

### Seam Strip Lock (Pixel-based for 1024×1536)

## Additional Notes

- **Foreground Elements**: Keep the foreground firmly **urban-core**: streets, podiums, station approaches, retail frontage, transport infrastructure, and dense building bases. Do not let it drift into residential-neighborhood language.
- **Urban Texture Balance**: Small pockets of greenery are allowed, but Tile 6 must remain overwhelmingly **urban and dry**, with no nature-dominant read and no open-water read.
- **Tile 7 Transition**: As **Tile 6** transitions into **Tile 7**, the **verticality** of the buildings should **slowly fade**, allowing for a transition into **more industrial and functional infrastructure** as the **airport zone** dominates.
