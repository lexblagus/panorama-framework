# Tile 5: Iconic Core (Anchor)

The heart of the city and the **ruler** for shared framing physics. Tile 5 defines peak density, warmth, and atmospheric diffusion for the entire panorama.

- **Density:** Ultra High
- **Form:** Maximum vertical pressure (telephoto-compressed; skyline crest near the upper frame boundary)
- **Atmosphere:** reflects the **lateral ambiance gradient** appropriate to this tile’s position and must not contradict the global progression
- **Lighting:** centered / frontal diffusion (no strong left/right key read)
- **FOV clamp:** rectilinear, strong telephoto compression, **narrow field of view**; forbid wide-angle drift or zoom-out framing

## Key Elements
- Tallest tower field and coherent skyline envelope (dense, layered, non-sterile)
- **Distributed skyline hierarchy:** prefer multiple comparable peaks over a single obvious hero spire
- **Dry urban core (hard):**
  - Tile 5 must remain **dry**.
  - No visible river, coastline, harbor, bay, canal, or distant open-water horizon read.
  - Do not allow far-background blue bands or reflective planes that read as water.
  - Any reflective surfaces must read as glass, roofs, paved surfaces, or urban infrastructure — never water.
- **Skyline envelope (Tile 5 signature):** a centered hump/crest that peaks near the middle and gently falls toward both sides (authoritative when an R1 map exists).
  - The crest should read as a broad central pressure field, not as a sharp crown, razor apex, or theatrical arch.
  - Avoid “magazine center-fold” behavior where all force collapses into one thin center line.
- **Reading orientation (Tile 5 signature):** dominant visual read is **bottom → top** (centered vertical pull toward the skyline crest).
- **Primary anchor zone doctrine:** highest-density “core” features live inside the primary anchor zone (when using an R1 map):
  - tallest tower cluster + densest vertical mass inside the anchor zone
  - avoid placing unique hero shapes inside seam blending strips
  - do not clip major towers at the left/right edges
- **Central-band discipline:** avoid a single uninterrupted, high-contrast corridor / rail canyon / traffic ribbon dominating the central vertical band; the center should read as **busy texture**, not a privileged spine
- **Circulation (maze-lattice, aligned to vertical read):**
  - city circulation reads as a distributed maze lattice (dense streets, intersections, service alleys) with intermittent elevated segments
  - **no** single giant freeway ribbon and **no** single dominant interchange
  - an implied axial “urban valley” / stacked street canyons / layered corridors may guide the eye upward,
    but avoid one clean uninterrupted highway running straight up the center; keep corridors frequently occluded/broken by blocks
- **Braided circulation logic:** corridor energy may read as a loose “V” orientation, but expressed through **bundles of adjacent paths**, not two clean hero avenues
- **Continuity breakers:** cross-cuts and short segments that prevent any “super ribbon” read
- **Edge-safe seam discipline:** left/right edges remain texture-only (short street fragments, rooftops, minor alleys); no major corridor exiting cleanly at seams; no landmark tower clipped at seams
- Prevent push-in reads by avoiding a single near-field avenue/interchange filling the bottom; rely on **midground layering**

## Depth & Layering (anti “push-in”)
- **Foreground:** textured rooftops, street hints, small vehicles (no huge near-field roadway filling the bottom).
- **Midground:** dense stacked blocks, mixed corridors, partial viaduct pieces, bridges, overpasses (short segments, not poster-ramps).
- **Background:** compressed skyline mass dissolving slightly with distance (haze primarily in far distance; preserve the “telephoto stack”).

## Element Diversity (distributed, not focal)
- Include multiple midground features simultaneously (spread out; not centered as landmarks):
  - at least one rail element (surface / trench) **and** one short elevated metro/tram segment (not edge-to-edge clean)
  - one embedded station/stop presence (platform canopies / vents / stair cores), integrated into blocks
  - infrastructure texture: rooftop mechanicals, antennas, water tanks, vents, pedestrian overpasses, utility bridges
  - small pocket plazas/terraces/roof gardens allowed (subtle; not nature-dominant)

## Architecture Diversity (force variety, avoid repetition)
- Show at least **four typological families simultaneously**:
  1) modern glass/steel towers with varied crowns/setbacks
  2) dense mid-rise residential with balconies
  3) brutalist/infrastructure volumes (concrete slabs/service structures)
  4) older brick/stone commercial blocks (subtle art-deco / neo-gothic hints)
- Avoid a single iconic spire or landmark silhouette; vary rooflines/facade rhythms.

## Stitch Discipline (hard rule)
- Left/right seam blending strips: keep low-uniqueness, repeatable texture only (haze, roof-fields, mid-rise repetition).
- No big clipped towers, no edge-cut megastructures, no edge-to-edge bridge/highway spans.
- Any major corridor must fade/fragment/occlude before reaching seam zones.

## Framing Stability Authority (Tile 5 is the ruler)
- Establish the shared vanishing-height feel and vertical “pressure” for all tiles; maintain telephoto compression and disciplined sky budget.
- If an **R1 composition map** exists for Tile 5, treat it as the authoritative layout control layer (not a “photo reference”): match its sky budget line, skyline envelope, horizon line height, reading orientation, edge-safe zones, seam blending zones, and primary anchor zone placement.
- Do **not** vertically recenter to fit towers; cropping/clipping is preferred if elements press the frame.
- If framing reads too open, add skyline mass, stacked midground slabs, or haze inside the distance volume instead of lifting framing.
- Skyline height must match the global horizon/vanishing-height reference (no independent pitch read).

## Sun Disk (Variant, Tile 5 only)
- **Default:** do **not** show a visible sun disk (amber peak via diffusion).
- **If explicitly enabled:** the solar disk may appear **only in Tile 5** (and the Central Master Reference), fully diffused through haze with **no hard edge**.
- The disk may be partially cropped; **never** lift framing to fit it.

## Forbidden Motifs
- Industrial decay
- Nature dominance, rural elements
- River/coastline/harbor/canal/open-water read
- Heavy industry

## Active Calibration Layer
- Lock the shared vanishing-height read and vertical pressure for all tiles; no independent up/down pitch.
- Cropping/clipping tower tips is acceptable; never lift framing or widen FOV to fit them.
- If sky feels too open, add skyline mass, stacked midground slabs, or haze inside the same framing.
- If the read feels push-in, reduce near-field dominance and add depth via midground stacking/overlap.
- **Enforcement (Tile 5 specific):** Tile 5 must not read “lower” than Tiles 3/7; correct by adding mass and density, not sky. Reject any framing that widens FOV or reduces apparent tower height relative to Tiles 3 and 7.
