# Tile 9: Heavy Industrial Expanse

Dense but industrial. Smokestacks, cargo lines, heavy machinery. City dissolves into haze.

- **Density:** Ultra High (infrastructure density)
- **Form:** Heavy industry. Heaviest infrastructure dissolving into haze, biased toward center and right half of frame.
- **Atmosphere:** reflects the **lateral ambiance gradient** appropriate to this tile’s position and must not contradict the global progression
- **Key Elements**
  - **Composition lock (DO NOT DRIFT):**
    - Dominant read is a **strong diagonal** running **bottom-left → top-right** (matches the panorama’s right-side bias).
    - The primary water body is a **navigable industrial river/channel** that occupies the **bottom-right quadrant**, hugs the **right edge**, and **recedes into the horizon** (distance-collapse into haze). Avoid a hard “L” turn that re-orients the scene.
    - The left bank (land side) is a continuous industrial frontage: docks, quays, straight seawalls, containment edges, and hard angular shoreline geometry.
  - **Engineered shoreline REQUIRED:** Land–water boundary must read as an engineered, industrial shoreline: predominantly straight, angular, and artificial, formed by docks, harbors, and containment structures. Organic, eroded, or natural coastlines are not allowed.
  - **Port + shipping (scale anchors):**
    - Include realistic port infrastructure: piers, cranes, mooring zones, container stacks, service roads, cargo trucks, empty container handlers (ECH).
    - Include **at least one utilitarian cargo ship/tanker** (correct scale). Prefer a ship presence near the **lower-right** without making it a “hero” centerpiece.
  - Ultra-dense heavy industry: refineries/petrochemical plants, stacks, pipe forests, conveyors, warehouses, power/utility infrastructure, railyards and track fans.
  - **Maximum haze mandate:** Tile 9 must exhibit the strongest atmospheric density in the panorama.
    - Visibility collapse: far background should be **nearly erased** into milky smog; skyline edges must not be crisp.
    - Haze may glow softly (volumetric radiance) but **must not** increase contrast or re-warm the scene globally.
  - **Color/grade constraint (anti-blue-drift):**
    - After Tile 5, warmth must not “return” as a global grade, but Tile 9 must also **avoid cyan/blue cast** drift.
    - Target palette: **desaturated polluted steel + dirty beige/soot-gray particulate** (cool-neutral overall, not “blue-hour”).
    - Any warmth may exist only as **tiny utilitarian sodium/work-light blooms** under haze (Tiles 8–9 exception), never as a global regrade.
  - **Light-direction enforcement:** key light from the **left**; primary cast shadows fall to the **right**.
  - Wind direction to right (affecting chimney smoke)
- **Forbidden Motifs**
  - Nature
  - Landmark buildings
  - Clear skyline edges must dissolve into haze
  - Residential streets
  - Sun prohibited (see _Global Constraints / Lighting_).
