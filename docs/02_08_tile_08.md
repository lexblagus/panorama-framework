# Tile 8: Industrial (Tertiary — Carrara Industrial Ridge)

Tile 8 extends the hard industrial mass of Tile 9 leftward **without inheriting the channel/ships**.
It is the “industrial wall” escalation tile: petrochemical + intermodal megacomplex, high infrastructure density, no residential identity.

## Core Identity
- **Theme:** Petrochemical + intermodal logistics megacomplex (no residential identity).
- **Primary read:** Dense industrial systems: pipe forests, racks, tanks, utility corridors, rail/yard grids.
- **Signature anchor:** One engineered **Carrara-like terraced quarry ridge**, fully industrialized.

## Hard Constraints (Non-negotiable)
- **No navigable channel. No ships/tankers.** Avoid prominent open water.
- If any reference suggests water on the right, **ignore the shape** for Tile 8; convert to dry industrial hardstand or small retention basins only.
- Any liquid may appear only as **small industrial retention basins** (non-compositional).
- **Lighting:** Key light from the **LEFT**; cast shadows fall to the **RIGHT**.
- **Wind:** Pushes smoke/steam/dust to the **RIGHT** (short, subtle, non-hero plumes).
- **Sun:** **Prohibited** (no visible sun disk).
- **Forbidden motifs:** parks/nature dominance, residential neighborhoods, landmark buildings, decorative signage, “city sparkle” nightlife.

## Air / Depth
- Tile 8 is late-right: **strong distance rolloff and distance collapse** (desaturation + contrast reduction with distance).
- **No uniform veil/blanket:** horizon remains legible; midground remains readable.
- **Avoid full whiteout:** far distance becomes ghost silhouettes; midground retains structure.
- Palette: polluted steel + tan/amber industrial dust tint; **not** clean golden sunlight.
- Tile 8 must stay in the same late-right atmospheric family as Tiles 7 and 9.
- The pale quarry must NOT bleach or cool the whole tile into a separate lighting world.
- Warmth may be muted and dusty, but the tile must still feel continuous with the industrial amber / dust / haze logic of its neighbors.

## Seam Strip Lock (Hard, pixel-based for 1024×1536)
- Leftmost **120px** and rightmost **120px** are **seam blending strips**: low-uniqueness repeatable industrial texture only.
- Forbidden inside seam strips: dominant quarry faces, big cooling towers, tall stacks, hero cranes, conveyor endpoints, flare tips, strong diagonals, or any long straight high-contrast line.
- No major corridor begins/ends cleanly on edges; lines curve/occlude internally.
- LEFT seam must feel visually compatible with Tile 7’s logistics/airport-adjacent atmosphere even though airport identity itself is forbidden.
- RIGHT seam must feel visually compatible with Tile 9’s industrial harbor atmosphere without copying channel/ship geometry.

## Required Anchor — Carrara Industrial Ridge (CENTERED, HARD)
- Place **one** dominant engineered Carrara-like terraced quarry ridge **CENTERED** in the tile.
- Ridge mass centroid should sit near the image center (x ≈ 512px).
- Keep ridge silhouette fully inside the edge-safe zone: stay within **x = 200..824** (never inside seam strips).
- Must read as human-sculpted quarry/cut ridge: bright chalky cut faces, clean terraces/benches, sharp bench lines, retaining walls.
- NO greenery. NOT natural mountains. NOT scenic.

### Ridge must be industrialized (NO empty buffer) — HARD
- The ridge is NOT isolated; it is colonized by industry on multiple terraces.
- NO wide empty apron/road band separating ridge from refinery systems.
- Terraces/benches are built-out industrial platforms packed with: pipe racks, catwalks, tank clusters, pump sheds, conveyor galleries,
  crushers/screening towers, substations/transformers, maintenance gantries.
- Industry climbs terraces: multiple pipe corridors and conveyors run **up and across** the ridge.
- Terrace population requirement: **60–80%** of visible benches contain industrial objects.

## Required Systems (Missing-piece locks)
- **Refinery node(s):** 1–2 dense refinery nodes in center-right/right-mid (not in seam strips): columns, megapipe-racks, cracking towers, exchangers, subtle flare stacks.
- **Power block:** turbine hall + dense substation/switchyard grid + transformers + pylons fading with distance rolloff (not in seam strips).
- **Intermodal rail dominance:** switching ladders/track fans, 2–4 long freight trains, container transfer yards, gantries, truck staging; break long rails before seams.
- Increase industrial core mass toward the RIGHT side of Tile 8 so the handoff into Tile 9 feels heavier and more industrial.
- Avoid keeping all identity concentrated at the tile center; the right half should carry substantial refinery / plant presence as well.

## Required Anchor — Covered Conveyor (Stitch-safe)
- One major covered conveyor in the midground.
- Runs mostly **LEFT–RIGHT** (near horizontal) or shallow arc; endpoints disappear behind structures.
- Must NOT run border-to-border; must NOT hit edges as strong diagonal.
