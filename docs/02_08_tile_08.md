# Tile 8: Industrial (Tertiary)

Tile 8 extends the hard industrial mass of Tile 9 leftward **without inheriting the channel/ships**.
It must remain stitch-safe on BOTH sides (Tile 7 ↔ Tile 9) and obey the global camera rig.

## Core Identity
- **Theme:** Petrochemical + intermodal logistics megacomplex (no residential identity).
- **Primary read:** Heavy infrastructure density with clustered refinery nodes and sprawling horizontal substrate.
- **Signature anchor:** A tall human-sculpted **Carrara-like terraced industrial ridge** (engineered quarry / cut ridge).

## Hard Constraints (Non-negotiable)
- **No navigable channel. No ships/tankers.** Avoid prominent open water.
- Any liquid may appear only as **small industrial retention basins** (non-compositional).
- **No visible sun disk.**
- **Lighting:** key light from the **LEFT**; shadows fall to the **RIGHT**.
- **Wind:** pushes smoke/steam/dust to the **RIGHT** (short, subtle, non-hero plumes).
- **Non-mirrored output:** do not flip horizontally in canonical runs.

## Air / Depth and Gradient
- Tile 8 is late-right: **strong distance rolloff / distance collapse** (desaturation + contrast reduction with distance).
- **No uniform veil/blanket:** horizon remains legible; midground remains readable.
- Avoid full whiteout: far distance becomes ghost silhouettes; midground retains structure.
- Palette: polluted steel + tan/amber dust tint (NOT clean “golden sun” look).

## Required Anchor — Carrara Industrial Ridge (REQUIRED)
A tall engineered ridge at **center-left / left-mid**:
- Bright chalky/limestone cut faces, **clean geometric terraces/benches**, sharp bench lines, retaining walls.
- Must read as **human-sculpted quarry / cut ridge** (Carrara-like), NOT natural mountains.
- **No greenery**; no scenic landscape cues.

### Ridge as Air/Depth Separator (Subtle)
- Ridge may anchor a local ambience gradient **without becoming a hard seam**:
  - **Far-left:** slightly clearer + slightly warmer (Tile 7-adjacent), still industrial (NOT Tile 5 amber).
  - **Right half:** heavier industrial dust tint + stronger distance collapse (Tile 8/9-adjacent).
- Wind is rightward: ridge can emit localized low-contrast dust drift to the **RIGHT** (no blanket).
- Must remain a **gradient**, not a binary split.

## Industrialized Terraces (HARD — Prevent “Empty Buffer”)
The ridge is colonized by industry:
- **No wide empty apron/road band** between ridge and refinery (no “dead zone”).
- Terraces/benches are built-out industrial platforms packed with:
  - pipe racks, catwalks, tank clusters, pump sheds, conveyor galleries
  - crushers/screening towers, substations/transformers, maintenance gantries
- Industry climbs terraces: multiple levels of pipe corridors and conveyors run **up and across** the ridge.
- Ridge roads are narrow service switchbacks only (secondary).

### Terrace population requirement (HARD)
- At least **60–80%** of visible benches contain industrial objects.
- Include at least two bench districts:
  1) crusher/screening + conveyor feed district
  2) tank/utility bench district with transformers/pylons/substation gear

## Required Anchor — Conveyor (Stitch-safe)
- One major **covered conveyor** in the midground.
- Runs mostly **LEFT–RIGHT** (near horizontal) or shallow arc; endpoints disappear behind structures.
- Must NOT run border-to-border; must NOT hit edges as a strong diagonal.

## Seam Strip Lock (Hard, pixel-based for 1024×1536)
- Leftmost **120px** and rightmost **120px** are **seam blending strips**: low-uniqueness repeatable industrial texture only.
- Forbidden inside seam strips: dominant quarry faces, big cooling towers, tall stacks, hero cranes, conveyor endpoints, strong diagonals, or any long straight high-contrast line.
- No major corridor begins/ends cleanly on edges; let lines curve/occlude internally.

## Stitch Control (Conceptual)
- Bias strongest vertical petrochemical mass toward **center/right** to ease stitch into Tile 9,
  but keep seam strips generic and low-uniqueness.
