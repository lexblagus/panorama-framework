# Tile 8: Industrial

Tile 8 extends the hard industrial mass of Tile 9 leftward **without inheriting the channel/ships**. The defining feature is a tall, human-sculpted **Carrara-like terraced ridge** that acts as a subtle ambience separator while remaining fully **industrialized** (no empty buffer zones).

- **Density:** Ultra High (infrastructure density)
- **Form:** Mixed dominance — sprawling horizontal substrate with clustered vertical refinery nodes
- **Primary read:** Petrochemical + logistics megacomplex; no residential identity

## Hard Constraints (Non-negotiable)
- **No navigable channel. No ships/tankers.** Avoid prominent open water.
  - Any liquid may appear only as **small industrial retention basins** (non-compositional).
- **Lighting:** Key light from the **LEFT**; primary cast shadows fall to the **RIGHT**
- **Wind:** Pushes smoke/steam/dust to the **RIGHT**
- **Sun:** **Prohibited** (no visible sun disk)
- **Forbidden motifs:** parks/nature dominance, residential neighborhoods, landmark buildings, decorative signage, “city sparkle” nightlife

## Air / Depth and Gradient
- Tile 8 is late-right: **strong distance rolloff and distance collapse** (depth conveyed by desaturation + contrast reduction with distance).
- **No uniform atmospheric veil/blanket**: horizon remains legible; midground remains readable.
- **Avoid full whiteout:** far distance becomes ghost silhouettes with softened edges; **midground remains readable** with soft edges and reduced contrast.
- Palette may be warm tan/amber due to industrial dust/aerosol tint, but must not read as clean “golden sun” lighting (no sunbeams, no crisp high-contrast warmth).

## Required Anchor — Carrara Industrial Ridge (REQUIRED)
A tall engineered ridge at **center-left / left-mid**:
- Bright chalky/limestone cut faces, **clean geometric terraces/benches**, sharp bench lines, retaining walls
- Must read as human-sculpted quarry / cut ridge (Carrara-like), **not** a natural mountain
- **No greenery**, no scenic landscape cues

### Ridge as Air/Depth Separator (Subtle)
- The ridge may anchor a local ambience gradient **without becoming a hard seam**:
  - **Far-left:** slightly clearer + slightly warmer (Tile 7-adjacent), but still polluted/industrial (NOT Tile 5 amber)
  - **Right half:** heavier industrial dust/aerosol tint + stronger distance rolloff / distance collapse (Tile 8/9-adjacent)
  - Wind is rightward: ridge can emit localized low-contrast dust/aerosol drift to the **RIGHT** (no uniform blanket)
  - Must remain a **gradient**, not a binary split

## Industrialized Terraces (HARD — Prevent “Empty Buffer”)
The ridge is not isolated. It is colonized by industry:
- **No wide empty apron/road band** separating ridge from industry (no “dead zone” buffer)
- Terraces/benches are **built-out industrial platforms** packed with:
  - pipe racks, catwalks, small tank clusters, pump sheds, conveyor galleries
  - crushers/screening towers, substations/transformers, maintenance gantries
- Industry climbs terraces: multiple levels of pipe corridors and conveyors run **up and across** the ridge, with bridge-like trusses and retaining-wall penetrations
- Any ridge roads are narrow service switchbacks only (secondary), never a broad empty corridor
- **Terrace population requirement:** at least **60–80% of visible benches** contain industrial objects (equipment, racks, tanks, sheds, conveyors, cranes, pylons)
- Include at least two distinct bench districts:
  1) crusher/screening + conveyor feed district
  2) tank/utility bench district with transformers/pylons/substation gear

## Required Anchor — Conveyor (REQUIRED, Stitch-safe)
- One major **covered conveyor** as a midground feature
- Must run **mostly LEFT–RIGHT** (near horizontal) or a shallow arc
- Endpoints must disappear behind structures
- Must **not** run border-to-border and must **not** hit image edges as a strong diagonal

## Industrial Content Palette (What it should contain)
- Petrochemical megacomplex: pipe-rack megagrids, catwalks, heat exchangers, pump yards, gantries, maintenance scaffolds
- **Stacks/chimneys limited to 2–3 clustered refinery nodes** (not uniform coverage)
- Tank farms: large cylinders + medium tanks + some spherical LPG tanks; containment berms and spill channels
- Power block: turbine hall, **cooling towers**, transformer yard / substation / switchyard, high-voltage pylons
- Intermodal logistics: container hardstands, container stacks, cranes/gantries, rail spurs, long cargo trains, switching/classification ladders
- Scrap/recycling and service substrate: checkpoints, fences, utility corridors, heavy truck staging, maintenance yards

## Stitch Control
- Bias strongest vertical petrochemical mass toward **center/right** to stitch into Tile 9.
- Keep outer **~8% of left/right edges** free of unique hero objects (dominant quarry face, big cooling towers, dominant conveyor).
- Edge zones should be generic repeating texture: pipe racks, low warehouses, rail spurs fading via distance rolloff.
- No major corridors begin/end cleanly on edges; let them fade/curve/dissolve internally.
