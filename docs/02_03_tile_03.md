# Tile 3: Transition Zone

## General Tile Identity
- Tile Name: Tile 3
- Tile Type: Secondary
- Role: Transitional urban system between Tile 1’s nature-dominant chaos and Tile 5’s cosmopolitan vertical intensity.
- Primary Visuals: inhabited, mixed, green-interwoven city fabric with growing urban density and infrastructural layering.

## Core Identity
- Tile 3 is the perceptual + semantic bridge between:
  - Tile 1 (nature-dominant chaos)
  - Tile 5 (cosmopolitan megacity core / ruler)
- It establishes urban complexity without monumentality.
- It introduces green urbanism + human-scale signals before vertical pressure escalates.
- It normalizes panorama scale by providing a mid-density reference.
- It is **not** an edge condition and **not** a pre-core skyline band.

## Density and Form
- Dense, continuous urban fabric
- Predominantly low- to mid-rise
- Density through accumulation, heterogeneity, and overlap (not height)
- Urban form is irregular, lived-in, layered
- Nature is interwoven, not scenic / separated

## Key Elements
- Canalized stream / river remnant:
  - concrete channel with softened, vegetated edges (non-heroic)
  - stitch-safe behavior (LOCAL, REQUIRED):
    - may enter from top / bottom
    - must curve, fragment, occlude, dissolve, or terminate internally
    - must not run cleanly into the left or right seam
    - no large linear element touching side edges
- Interwoven green urbanism:
  - trees within blocks
  - planted medians
  - weedy vegetation along infrastructure
- Mixed residential fabric:
  - mid-rise apartment blocks
  - smaller houses
  - heterogeneous ages and styles
- Subtle architectural plurality is encouraged inside the residential mix:
  - a few Chinese- or Japanese-influenced houses
  - compact courtyard-like compounds
  - tiled roof silhouettes
  - narrow low-rise mixed-use shop houses
- These should remain scattered and embedded into the ordinary neighborhood fabric.
- They must not form a themed enclave or become a dominant visual motif.
- Human-scale circulation:
  - pedestrian stairways
  - footbridges
  - small overpasses
- Neighborhood commerce:
  - market strips
  - corner shops
  - small ground-floor retail
- Rooftop life:
  - water tanks
  - laundry lines
  - small rooftop structures
- Pocket parks / courtyards (subtle, secondary)
- Embedded transit:
  - small subway entrances
  - short elevated rail segments
  - non-monumental stations

## Atmosphere and Visibility
- Tiles 3–4 warm up toward amber, but visibility stays high
- No fog veil / no milky smog blur
- Depth haze allowed only as subtle distance rolloff

## Forbidden Motifs
- Iconic skyline silhouettes
- Landmark towers / heroic vertical forms
- Distant high-rise skyline band
- Large scenic parks / landscaped masterplans
- Stadiums / civic megastructures
- Purely residential suburbia
- Purely infrastructural dominance (belongs to Tile 7)
- Pristine / untouched nature (belongs to Tile 1)
- Themed ethnic-quarter postcard imagery; hero pagoda / shrine / ceremonial gate motifs
