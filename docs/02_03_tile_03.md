# Tile 3: Transition Zone

## Canonical Human Spec

### Description
Tile 3 depicts a **transitional urban system** between Tile 1’s nature-dominant chaos and Tile 5’s cosmopolitan vertical intensity.
It remains **inhabited, mixed, and green-interwoven**, but urban density and infrastructural layering are clearly asserting control.
This is **not** an edge condition and **not** a pre-core skyline band.

### Role
- Acts as the **perceptual + semantic bridge** between:
  - Tile 1 (nature-dominant chaos)
  - Tile 5 (cosmopolitan megacity core / ruler)
- Establishes **urban complexity without monumentality**
- Introduces **green urbanism + human-scale signals** before vertical pressure escalates
- Normalizes panorama scale by providing a **mid-density reference**

### Density & Form
- Dense, continuous urban fabric
- Predominantly **low- to mid-rise**
- Density through **accumulation, heterogeneity, overlap** (not height)
- Urban form is **irregular, lived-in, layered**
- Nature is **interwoven**, not scenic / separated

### Key Elements
- **Canalized stream / river remnant**:
  - concrete channel with softened, vegetated edges (non-heroic)
  - **stitch-safe behavior (LOCAL, REQUIRED):**
    - may enter from top/bottom
    - must **curve, fragment, occlude, dissolve, or terminate internally**
    - must **not** run cleanly into the **left or right seam**
    - no large linear element touching side edges
- Interwoven green urbanism inside the fabric:
  - trees within blocks
  - planted medians
  - weedy vegetation along infrastructure
- Mixed residential fabric:
  - mid-rise apartment blocks + smaller houses
  - heterogeneous ages and styles
- Human-scale circulation:
  - pedestrian stairways
  - footbridges
  - small overpasses
- Neighborhood commerce:
  - market strips
  - corner shops
  - small ground-floor retail
- Rooftop life:
  - water tanks
  - laundry lines
  - small rooftop structures
- Pocket parks / courtyards (subtle, secondary)
- Embedded transit:
  - small subway entrances
  - short elevated rail segments
  - non-monumental stations

### Atmosphere / Visibility (Tile-position accurate)
- Tiles 3–4 warm up toward amber, but **visibility stays high**
- **No fog veil / no milky smog blur**; depth haze is allowed only as subtle distance rolloff

### Forbidden Motifs
- Iconic skyline silhouettes
- Landmark towers / heroic vertical forms
- Distant high-rise skyline band
- Large scenic parks / landscaped masterplans
- Stadiums / civic megastructures
- Purely residential suburbia
- Purely infrastructural dominance (belongs to Tile 7)
- Pristine / untouched nature (belongs to Tile 1)

  - vehicles, shops, stairs, rooftops act as scale references

  - crowding through variety and overlap, not height
  - taller elements (if any) remain small, rare, and subordinate

- Avoid drift toward:
  - suburban calm
  - skyline formation
  - infrastructural dominance

  - increase repetition of mid-rise slabs, roof clutter, and layered infrastructure so the city mass occupies more of the frame **without becoming a skyline**
