# Tile 1: Nature as Dominant Chaos
The **natural extreme** of the panorama, expressed as a rugged Mediterranean/Italian-style **coastline wedge** (“/”, not “V”).

- **Topology (signature):** RIGHT side is dominant cliff/hillside landmass rising to the RIGHT; LEFT side is open sea/inlet negative space (quiet, stitch-friendly).
- **Composition lock:** envelope slope rises toward the RIGHT; dominant diagonal read is bottom-right → top-left (when R1 exists, it is the authority).
- **Density:** Very low (non-urban)
- **Form:** Terrain-dominant, vertically aggressive
- **Atmosphere:** Clear, crisp daylight; strong material definition; respects lateral gradient
- **Lighting (target):** key light from FAR RIGHT; cast shadows fall LEFT (best-effort; do not destabilize framing to “fix” it). No sun disk

## Key Elements
- Steep cliffs, exposed rock, rugged elevation changes (RIGHT)
- Coastal river/stream + narrow estuary/inlet/reservoir meeting the sea **without** forming a centered V-valley
- Cascades/waterfall/spillway; **optional** cliff-integrated dam/reservoir step (visible controlled drop)
- Dense irregular vegetation variety (Mediterranean shrubs/pines + mixed hillside growth)
- Infrastructure as cuts/scars (NOT grid): **stacked coastal road system** carved into rock
  - show 2–3 roadway traces (lower coastline road + mid-slope contour road + occasional upper viaduct fragment)
  - 2–4 tunnel mouths at different heights; short viaduct spans; retaining walls/switchbacks
  - roads should be partially occluded by vegetation/rock (avoid a single clean highway ribbon)
- **Optional passenger rail cue (subordinate):** short hillside segment or tunnel mouth + brief viaduct hugging terrain (not a hero corridor)
- **Optional funicular/teleférico cue (one only):** small, realistic scale, subordinate detail
- More hillside settlements (still non-urban): terraced village pockets + hillside houses + a few mansions/villas
- Interconnection cues: stairways, footpaths, small pedestrian bridges, terraces, retaining walls, switchback alleys
- **Caves/grottoes encouraged:** sea caves/cliff cavities as secondary detail
- **Road read:** coastline road is a major leading line entering near lower-right and receding toward top-left.
- **Sea negative space:** keep left-side water field clean (no clutter of many boats/structures).
- **Stitch safety:** LEFT seam is open water only; RIGHT seam is repeatable rock/vegetation + small non-unique buildings; no edge-clipped hero objects; no large linear element touching side edges.

## Forbidden Motifs
- Symmetric V-shaped valley / centered river canyon composition
- Skylines / distant megacity silhouettes; high-rises; urban grids/major avenues
- Pastoral suburbia; flat/gently rolling landscapes; sun disk/visible solar source
- Beach resort/marina/yachts clutter on the LEFT sea side (keep sea as quiet negative space)
